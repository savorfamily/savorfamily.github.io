우르부르르wurubururu — a desk notification device for Gmail, Google Calendar and your websites

우르부르르 (wurubururu) is a free, open-source desktop app for macOS and Windows that brings the Wuruburu USB internet-radio toy (Ddanzi, 2001) back to life. When a new email or an upcoming event arrives, the toy's ears blink. Press its foot to open the message, or have it read the title aloud.

Download for macOS / Windows Source code (GitHub) Privacy policy

What it does

New Gmail messages

The ears blink when a new unread message arrives in your inbox. Messages Gmail marks as Important or Starred blink red; others blink green. Promotions and Social tabs are skipped.

Upcoming calendar events

A reminder a few minutes before each event on your primary Google Calendar, and again when it starts.

Foot buttons

Left foot opens the message or event in your browser. Right foot reads its title aloud through the toy's speaker. The head button dismisses alerts.

Website monitoring and other alerts

Optional checks for your own websites, focus timer, internet radio, and alerts from build or deploy scripts.

How Google user data is used

To show notifications, 우르부르르 asks for two read-only Google permissions. You sign in with Google from the app's settings screen and can disconnect at any time.

PermissionWhat the app readsWhy
gmail.metadataSender, subject, labels and IDs of new unread inbox messages. Never the message body or attachments.To blink the ears for new mail, choose red or green from Gmail's own Important/Starred labels, read the subject aloud, and open the message in Gmail.
calendar.events.readonlyTitle, start time and link of upcoming events on your primary calendar.To remind you before an event starts and open it in Google Calendar.

Everything stays on your computer. The app talks directly to Google from your own computer. There is no developer server; Google user data is never sent to, stored on, or seen by the developer.

Never sent to AI. Gmail and Calendar data is never sent to any AI service, and is never used to develop, improve, or train AI or machine-learning models.

Read-only. The app cannot send, change, or delete your mail or events.

우르부르르's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the privacy policy.

Requirements

macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel), or Windows 11. A Wuruburu USB device is needed for the lights and buttons; without it the app can still show alerts in its settings screen.

우르부르르는

2001년 딴지일보가 만든 인터넷 라디오 단말 우르부르를 요즘 맥과 윈도우에서 다시 쓰는 무료 오픈소스 프로그램입니다. 새 메일이나 다가오는 일정이 있으면 귀가 깜빡이고, 발 버튼을 누르면 메일을 열거나 제목을 읽어줍니다.

Google 정보 사용

  • Gmail(머리글 전용): 새로 받은 메일의 보낸 사람·제목·라벨만 봅니다. 본문과 첨부는 읽지 않습니다.
  • 캘린더(읽기 전용): 기본 캘린더의 다가오는 일정 제목·시작 시각·링크만 봅니다.
  • 내 컴퓨터 안에서만: 개발자 서버가 없고, Google 정보는 개발자에게 전송·저장되지 않습니다.
  • AI로 보내지 않음: Gmail·캘린더 정보는 어떤 AI 서비스로도 보내지 않으며, AI 학습에 쓰이지 않습니다.

우르부르르가 Google API로 받은 정보의 사용과 다른 앱으로의 전송은 Limited Use 요건을 포함한 Google API 서비스 사용자 데이터 정책을 따릅니다. 자세한 내용은 개인정보처리방침을 보세요.
